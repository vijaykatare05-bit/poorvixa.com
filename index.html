Global Helpdesk

Frequently Asked Questions

Read simple answers regarding international trade processes and supply commitments.

Q1: Which countries do you export to?
We regularly export agricultural products to active ports across the Middle East and Southeast Asia, including the United Arab Emirates (UAE), Saudi Arabia, Oman, Qatar, Bangladesh, Malaysia, and Singapore.
Q2: What agricultural products do you supply?
We specialize in premium Citrus fruits (Fresh Oranges, Mosambi), fresh bananas (G9 Cavendish), organic mangoes (Kesar & Alphonso), high-viability seeds, compost and nutrient fertilizers, bulk grains (wheat, rice), premium pulses, and eco-certified organic farm products.
Q3: Do you provide bulk orders?
Yes, our sourcing volume capability allows us to support weekly and monthly bulk commitments. We contract reefer and dry containers to ensure consistent supply throughout harvest seasons.
Q4: What certifications do your products have?
Our operations are certified by government authorities. We hold active registrations with APEDA (RCMC/APEDA/29766/2026-2027), DGFT Import Export Code (IEC), and GST. We supply authorized Phytosanitary Clearance, GlobalG.A.P., and ISO 9001 compliance sheets on client request.
Q5: How can I request a quotation?
You can fill out the RFQ (Request for Quote) form on this page or tap the floating WhatsApp button to chat directly with our trade team. Please mention sizes, packing requirements, and destination ports to get quick quotes.
Q6: What is the minimum order quantity?
To guarantee price feasibility and secure correct temperature control (cold chain reefer container limits), our minimum order quantity (MOQ) for fresh fruits is generally 1 Full Container Load (FCL - 20ft or 40ft Reefer depending on product shelf life).
